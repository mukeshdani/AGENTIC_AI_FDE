The Evolution of AI: 1950 to 2026+

Foundations (1950–1980s):

1950 (Alan Turing): Turing Test introduced the core question: "Can machines think?"
1956 (John McCarthy): Coined the term "Artificial Intelligence" at Dartmouth Conference.
Rule-Based Era: Pure symbolic AI & manual if-else rules. Scalability bottleneck occurred because human edge cases are infinite.

Statistical Learning & Breakthroughs (1990s–2016):

1997 (Deep Blue): IBM defeated World Chess Champion Garry Kasparov using brute-force game-tree search.
Machine Learning Era: Shift from Manual Rules 
→
→ Learning from Examples (Data + Labels). Bottleneck: Manual Feature Engineering.
2012 (AlexNet): Deep CNNs trained on GPUs (Nvidia) over ImageNet won by a massive margin. Computer Vision was born (Face Unlock, Medical Imaging).
2016 (AlphaGo & Move 37): DeepMind used Reinforcement Learning (Self-Play). In Game 2, "Move 37" (1 in 10,000 human probability) demonstrated emergent intuition and creativity beyond human dogma.

Language Revolution (2017–2022):

Sequential NLP Limits: Bag-of-Words (lost order), RNNs (Vanishing Gradient / short-term memory), LSTMs (memory gates helped, but sequential processing couldn't scale on GPUs).
2017 (Transformers): Google’s "Attention Is All You Need". Replaced recurrent loops with Self-Attention and enabled complete parallelism across GPUs.
LLMs: Transformers scaled on trillions of web tokens. Emergent properties appeared via Scaling Laws.
Nov 2022 (ChatGPT): Added RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) to align raw LLM outputs to human intent and conversational safety.

Modern Frontier (2024–2026+):

Multimodal AI: Native integration of Text, High-Resolution Vision, Real-Time Audio, and Video in unified embedding spaces.
The Limits of Pure LLMs: Knowledge cutoff, Hallucinations, lack of proprietary enterprise data, zero traceability.
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation): The bridge decoupling parametric memory from non-parametric external knowledge bases (Vector DBs, Hybrid Search, Re-ranking).
Agentic AI & Orchestration: Shift from Chatbots to Autonomous Agents (ReAct loop: Think 
→
→ Tool Call 
→
→ Observe 
→
→ Act) orchestrated via multi-agent systems (LangGraph, CrewAI) and standardized by protocols like MCP (Model Context Protocol).
